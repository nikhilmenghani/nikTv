bgutils.js bundles BotGuardClient, WebPoMinter and their official helpers
using esbuild 0.25.12, IIFE format, Chrome 90 target.
Local adaptation removes the upstream API key constant and header. NikTV's
native bridge supplies the build-configured key only to GenerateIT requests.
proof.js and the Kotlin bridge integrate normal service attestation for
public-video playback. The WebView only performs attestation; Media3
fetches and decodes all video and audio.

Player n-parameter preparation uses the yt-dlp/ejs solver (Unlicense),
Meriyah 6.1.4 (MIT) and Astring 1.9.0 (MIT). Bundles are taken from
https://github.com/yuliskov/MediaServiceCore at commit
c00b74d758c53f73aca5604a022b6de5bbfe6072, youtubeapi/src/main/assets/
nsigsolver. Original bundle copyright/SPDX headers are retained;
dependency license texts accompany the bundles.
